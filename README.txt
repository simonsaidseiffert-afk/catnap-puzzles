Catnap Puzzles 

A small, relaxing cat-themed logic puzzle game.

The goal is simple: place one cat in every row, column, and colored region. Cats can't touch each other, not even diagonally.

Every puzzle is designed to be solvable using logic rather than guessing.

 Features

- Three difficulties: Easy (5×5), Medium (7×7), and Hard (10×10)
- Randomly generated puzzles
- Logic hints
- Light and dark mode
- Sound effects
- Win streaks and personal best times
- Offline support
- No ads, accounts, or purchases

## How to play

Open the game in your browser.

- Click / tap: Mark or unmark a square with an X
- Double click / double tap: Place a cat
- Drag: Cross off multiple squares at once

On iPhone, you can add the game to your Home Screen through Safari.
